# Decoding faba bean yield stability

**From in-field image data to mechanistic models (DIGIFABA)**

DIGIFABA combines in-field and drone imagery with agronomy, statistics and machine learning to identify faba bean traits linked to stable yields under drought, supporting breeding for a changing climate.

The project is hosted at the University of Copenhagen and funded by the Novo Nordisk Foundation Data Science Collaborative Research Programme.
