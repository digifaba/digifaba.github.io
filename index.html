Beans that keep their yield when the rain doesn't come

DIGIFABA turns images of faba bean plants into knowledge breeders can use.

Discover the project See first results

The problem

Faba bean is rich in protein, but drought during flowering makes its yield unreliable.

The idea

Plants show their stress. We teach computers to see it, measure it and model it.

The payoff

Better traits to breed for, and steadier harvests of a climate-friendly protein.
